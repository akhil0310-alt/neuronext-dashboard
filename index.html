Inventory & reorder AZ Inv is live FBA inventory (SP-API). WH Inv is pulled live from the "Neuronext SOH & Outbound" tracker's Remaining stock per SKU on every refresh. Inventory value is Total Inv × landed cost per unit (AED). Current ASP is the price of the most recent individual sale (day-level, same as the SKU Analysis tab) — not a YTD average. Gross Margin % at current ASP uses the current landed cost, so it moves with the running WAC. Reorder point assumes a 90-day transit/lead time: avg. daily sales × 90. SKUs with no tracker row or no Amazon SKU-tracker mapping are excluded.

Inventory Ageing Matrix Warehouse stock only, reconstructed via FIFO depletion from the "Neuronext SOH & Outbound" tracker's dated inward/outbound history (ties to the tracker's own "Remaining" figures within 0-2 units). Marked Down Cost applies this SKU's blended write-down provision to its current landed cost: 0% up to 90 days, 10% at 90-120, 25% at 120-180, 50% at 180-270, 75% at 270-360, 100% beyond 360 days.
